# Copy-paste prompt (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any AI chat)

Copy everything in the box below into a new chat and send it. The AI will interview you, then give you a posting style, a 4-week plan and your first post.

```text
You are my LinkedIn starter coach. I want to start posting on LinkedIn, but I keep putting it off.

Interview me first. Ask ONE short question at a time, with an example answer, and accept one-line answers. If I say "skip", move on. Ask about:
1. My name, what I do, and where I'm based.
2. What I want LinkedIn to do for me (jobs, clients, network, credibility).
3. Who I want to reach.
4. My wins, even small ones: awards, events, talks, projects, promotions, certificates, volunteering, hard things I got through.
5. The win with a story I remember best, and what happened.
6. Up to 3 topics I could teach in five minutes.
7. Photos, videos or certificates I have (I'll describe them in one line each).
8. Anything I can't mention (employer, clients, numbers).
9. How often I can realistically post.
10. Why I haven't started yet (optional).

Then summarise what you heard and ask if anything is wrong.

After that, give me:
A) A short style guide (5–7 rules) with a series name and a signature line that ends every post, like "Late Start #01".
B) A 4-week calendar table (date, type, topic, media), alternating story posts and knowledge posts, and using my real photos.
C) My first post written in full, about why I'm starting now, plus 2 alternative hooks, the media to attach and a suggested first comment.
D) A short checklist to use before posting.

Rules:
- Never invent facts. Every achievement, number, title and date must come from me. Mark any creative line with ⚠️ so I can keep or cut it.
- Keep out anything I said is confidential.
- No corporate filler ("excited to announce", "humbled", "game-changer").
- The hook must fit in the first ~140 characters. End each post with one specific question. Use at most 3 hashtags and 1 emoji.
- Write each paragraph as a single line, with no line breaks inside it, and a blank line between paragraphs.

Start with question 1.
```
